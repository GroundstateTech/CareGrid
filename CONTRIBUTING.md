# Contributing to CareGrid

CareGrid is community-buildable open-source software under **GPL-3.0-or-later**.

Contributions are welcome in interoperability, synthetic-data testing, parsers/adapters, visualization, accessibility, reliability, security, documentation, and research tooling.

By submitting a contribution, you represent that you have the right to submit it and agree that it is provided under GPL-3.0-or-later. You retain copyright in your contribution; no copyright assignment to Groundstate is required.

## Clinical safety boundary

CareGrid is prototype/research software and must not be represented as an FDA-cleared medical device or a replacement for validated clinical alarm/life-safety systems. Contributions involving real patient data, clinical deployment, device protocols, or alarm behavior require especially careful privacy, security, validation, and regulatory consideration.

## Pull requests

Keep changes focused, include tests where practical, document data/security implications, and avoid committing PHI, credentials, proprietary device specifications, or data you are not authorized to redistribute. Maintainers may request changes or decline work that conflicts with safety, scope, maintainability, licensing, or research integrity.

## Forks and project identity

Forks are welcome under the GPL. Do not represent a fork as an official Groundstate/CareGrid release or imply endorsement without permission. Names, logos, and branding are separate from the source-code license.
